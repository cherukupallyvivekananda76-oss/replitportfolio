# Evidence in Motion — director's script

## Treatment

- **Format:** 4:5 portrait, 25 seconds, silent-usable with one instrumental bed. No narration.
- **Promise:** This is a portfolio where AI claims are connected to inspectable evidence, not inflated outcomes.
- **Identity:** Match the existing evaluation-lab site: deep pine `#101b1a`, panel `#172523`, acid lime `#c9f36a`, aqua `#7dd9c2`, warm off-white. Manrope is the display/body face; DM Mono labels the evidence system.
- **Hero:** A moving evidence line: it begins as the site’s small `V.` mark, branches into capability paths, becomes a project record and source citation, then resolves back into the mark.
- **Motion:** Stiff typographic snaps, SVG line-tracing, dimensional card reflow and a lime aperture. Avoid repeating generic slide entrances.
- **Arc:** Immediate hook → systematic evidence map → concrete project records → trustworthy ambassador → restrained identity lockup.
- **Payoff:** A full lime field replaces the dark lab interface for the closing identity, then its small square contracts to the opening seed.
- **Brand/assets:** Existing website is the brand source. No third-party logos, portrait, fabricated metrics, or generated imagery. UI-like cards are motion graphics built from verified site copy, not clickable controls or screenshots of a live conversation.

## Shot 01 / 0.00–4.00 s (4.00 s) — “Make AI useful.”

- **Purpose:** State the site's practical claim immediately, then challenge it with evidence.
- **Exact on-screen copy:** `V.` at 0.05 s; `MAKE AI` at 0.46 s; `USEFUL.` at 0.91 s; `MAKE ITS OUTPUT` at 1.70 s; `HOLD UP.` at 2.25 s; a small `AI GENERALIST / EVALUATION LAB` label at 3.05 s. All headline copy stays legible through 3.55 s. No VO.
- **Composition/artistic idea:** A small lime square at the upper-left safe edge becomes a wide lime baseline and then the second headline line. Oversized white type owns the left/center 70%; `HOLD UP.` is lime. A faint technical grid recedes behind type; rightmost 15% remains open for the rule to travel. No small floating product object.
- **Choreography:** 0.05 square snaps at scale 0.6→1.0; 0.46 and 0.91 lines reveal from a vertical mask in two distinct impacts; 1.45 the first headline compacts upward while a lime bar stretches across; 1.70 new line unmasks; 2.25 `HOLD UP.` pushes forward and settles; 3.05 label enters and a scan rule travels down the words; 3.50–4.00 the rule branches into three vertical paths while type shears out. Visible beats at <0.5 s intervals early, then at 3.05/3.50.
- **Transition contract:** The lime rule and V. square remain visible at the cut. The outgoing type exits as the incoming evidence paths are already drawing; no blank color frame.
- **Technique/assets:** CSS masks and transforms, SVG rule and deterministic grid. Existing site mark represented as text `V.`; no external logo asset.
- **Sound intent:** Minimal electronic pulse with a crisp opener accent. Beat alignment unverified until music exists.

## Shot 02 / 4.00–9.00 s (5.00 s) — “Three capabilities. Inspectable evidence.”

- **Purpose:** Translate the site's role-fit map into a visual system instead of listing generic skills.

These are labels, not claims of measured results.
- **Composition/artistic idea:** Three thick horizontal tracks step down a portrait frame, with a thin vertical lime spine left and a changing aqua node on each track. The first track fills the upper half, then the camera pulls back to show all three; evidence tags occupy the right/lower safe zones one at a time, never as a dense stack beside a headline.
- **Choreography:** 4.00 matching rule already in frame; 4.38 first track traces and node locks; 5.00 camera eases back, revealing space for second; 5.38 second track grows with a crisp joint; 6.38 third track folds out; 7.25 the spine sends pulses to evidence tags at 7.25/7.70/8.15; 8.48 final node grows to 1.55x and the surrounding tracks fold behind it.
- **Transition contract:** The enlarged final node becomes the lime corner index on the first project record. Dark field stays continuous while node scale and card depth overlap.
- **Technique/assets:** SVG path draw, CSS grid, Framer Motion camera scale and node transforms. No image asset required.
- **Sound intent:** Three distinct rhythmic accents for the tracks, one lighter connective tick per evidence tag. Alignment unverified.

- **Sound intent:** Low percussive page-turn-like accents, not literal paper sounds. Beat alignment unverified.

## Variety / safety review

The five major shots change scale and composition: close typography → diagram → dimensional records → editorial source field → bright centered identity. The lime rule/node/V. supplies continuity without repeating one layout. Each shot has an internal action after entrance and a late handoff beat; all scene start frames contain a recognizable element. No private contact details, unverified outcomes, interactive controls inside the film, or fabricated product footage are included.
