description: The chosen visual and information-hierarchy direction for the public recruiter portfolio.
---

Use the evaluation-lab direction for the public site: capability-to-evidence connections should be visible in the initial scan, with project status and the public contact action easy to find. The editorial direction is a retained exploration, not the production direction.

**Why:** The evaluation-lab concept made role alignment and inspectable evidence visible together in the first viewport, which better serves a recruiter’s quick scan. The editorial concept was warm and readable but deferred project proof farther down the page.

**How to apply:** Preserve the refined evidence-first hierarchy and responsive contact prominence when building the site. Replace all mockup-only interactions with functional implementations; do not treat the mockup’s scripted chat as a production answer source.
